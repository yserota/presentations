Getting There — Together

Four cross-organizational AI programs where TW is acting as a platform, not just a production team.

🛠 Supportability  ·  Customer Support
→ In progress
Closing Gaps from High-Impact Cases
High-priority support cases surface documentation failures. We are building a systematic loop: Support signals the gap, TW closes it.
Customer pain becomes a prioritized doc work item, not an anecdote.
🧰 Supportability AI  ·  Knowledge Management
◈ POC
AI-Powered KB → Docs Gap Analysis
AI analyzes the KB corpus, detects what customers are learning from support articles instead of official docs, and surfaces actionable documentation gaps.
Support knowledge systematically migrated into the docs layer, where it scales.
🤖 PANDA  ·  Palo Alto Networks Digital Assistant
→ In progress
TW Docs Ingested into PANDA
Collaborating with the PANDA team to ingest Idira documentation into PANDA’s knowledge base — enabling the company’s agentic AI assistant to answer employee questions from authoritative, maintained docs.
Documentation reaches employees at the moment of need — through AI, not search.
🌍 Marketplace  ·  Product & Ecosystem
→ In progress
AI Ecosystem for Plugin Content
An AI-assisted publishing ecosystem for internal and external teams to create, validate, and publish plugin documentation. TW defines the standards; AI enforces them at scale.
Quality extends beyond TW to the entire partner and developer ecosystem.
The pattern across all four
AI is the mechanism that makes organizational scale possible — not by removing human judgment, but by removing the manual overhead that prevents it from reaching every corner of the product.
